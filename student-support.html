Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common student questions.

Open the Assignment page, select the pending assignment, upload your file and submit it. Your submission status will then appear in the assignment list.
Go to the Resources section. You can browse notes, PDFs, Figma files, templates, videos and other class materials.
Open the Attendance page from the student portal navigation. You can view your overall percentage, calendar and recent attendance history.
Open the Result page to see your overall score, module performance, recent results and current grade.
Submit a support request using the form on this page and describe the problem clearly. Include the page name and what happened.
